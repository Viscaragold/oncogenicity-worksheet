Scope. This SOP covers somatic single nucleotide variants and small insertions or deletions, including deletions within a single gene. It is not for germline cancer predisposition variants, fusions, copy number variants or other chromosomal rearrangements.

Use alongside, not instead of, clinical actionability assessment. Oncogenicity and AMP/ASCO/CAP tier are different questions. Classify oncogenicity first, then assess actionability.

This worksheet does not assess evidence. It records your assessment, performs the arithmetic and drafts the wording. Every judgement remains yours, and routine clinical decisions should not be made on variants of uncertain significance.
